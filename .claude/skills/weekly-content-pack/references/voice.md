## Rules

**Do:**
- One idea per tweet.
- Short lines. Fragments are fine.
- Concrete nouns and real numbers over adjectives.
- Say "I" — this is his work.
- Admit what didn't work, without self-flagellation.

**Don't:**
- No LLM register: "delve", "unlock", "game-changer", "leverage", "in today's
  fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y".
- No rule-of-three rhythm in every tweet. It reads synthetic fast.
- No motivational close. A soft CTA is not a pep talk.
- No em-dash-heavy rhythm unless his tweets show it.
- No emoji unless his tweets show it. (UNCONFIRMED — ask.)
- Sentence case vs lowercase: UNCONFIRMED — ask.
- Don't explain the joke. Don't restate the hook in the close.
