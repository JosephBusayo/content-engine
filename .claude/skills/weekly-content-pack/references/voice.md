## What this tells us

**Structure**
- Sentence case. Normal capitalisation. **Not lowercase.**
- Blank line between almost every idea. White space is load-bearing.
- Two-line posts are common and they work. Not everything is a thread.
- Verdict or claim first, reasoning after. He does not build up to a point.
- Fragments are fine. "The copy is terrible." is a whole thought.

**Emoji**
- Essentially none, with one exception: **🧵 to signal a thread**. Use that and
  nothing else unless he says otherwise.

**Hashtags**
- Zero across every sample. Never add one.

**Tone**
- Blunt and willing to be negative: "the absolute worst", "Both designs are
  terrible." Don't sand this down into something diplomatic.
- Dry rather than excited. No exclamation marks in any sample.
- Analogies drawn from ordinary life (two legs, walking), not from tech.
- He tags the company he's talking about (@OPay_NG, @fiverr).
- Quote-tweets a story and adds one line. That *is* a post.

**Recurring position** — worth building on, it's already his:
> The tool isn't the problem, the prompting and the thinking are.

**Register**
- Plain words. He never reaches for an impressive one.
- Writes to one person.
- Small typos appear in the wild ("layed off", "way better that this"). Don't
  imitate typos — but don't over-polish either. A slightly rough line in his
  rhythm beats a clean line in nobody's.

## From his voice transcripts

He often talks to Claude by voice. Those show how he speaks: direct,
conversational, thinking out loud, plain words, practical Nigerian context.
Useful as a reference — but **tweets must be tighter than his speech**. Strip
the speech scaffolding ("so", "all right", "like I said"); it isn't voice, it's
thinking out loud.

## Reality check on reach

212 followers. Posts land between 7 and 83 views. This account is early.

So: no "as I always say", no posts written for an audience that isn't there yet,
no fake authority. Posts that assume a crowd read the last one will feel hollow.
Each post stands alone. Growth content, not legacy content.

## Don't

- No LLM register: "delve", "unlock", "game-changer", "leverage", "in today's
  fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y".
- No rule-of-three rhythm in every tweet. Reads synthetic immediately.
- No motivational close. A soft CTA is not a pep talk.
- No em-dash-heavy rhythm — his samples barely use them.
- Don't explain the joke. Don't restate the hook in the close.
- Don't make him sound like a thought leader. He sounds like a guy with an
  opinion and receipts.
